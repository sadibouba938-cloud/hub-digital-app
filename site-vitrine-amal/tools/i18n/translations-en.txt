221|Frequently asked questions
222|You may be wondering…
223|When will prices and technical datasheets be available?
224|The AMAL range is being finalised: public prices, technical datasheets and final photos of every product will be published on this page. In the meantime, all prices are quoted on request: fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp and we will reply within 24 working hours with the available sizes and the price of your order.
225|What is the minimum order quantity?
226|There is no minimum for individuals: you can buy a single bottle. To benefit from wholesale prices, orders start at 100 litres (or a mixed pallet of 20 cartons). Above 500 litres, an additional volume discount applies.
227|Can you manufacture under my own brand name?
228|Yes, that is our contract manufacturing service (white label). You give us your logo and brand name; we produce, label and pack in your formats. The minimum quantity for a custom production is usually 200 litres per reference — we will discuss it together depending on the product.
229|What are the delivery times and costs?
230|Dakar, Diamniadio, Rufisque and Bargny: delivery within 24 h. Thiès, Mbour, Saly, Kaolack, Touba, Diourbel: 48 to 72 h. Other regions are served on group orders. Costs depend on distance and volume: from 300 litres, delivery is free in Dakar and its suburbs.
231|Which payment methods do you accept?
232|Wave, Orange Money, Free Money, bank transfer (BNDE, CBAO, Ecobank), cheque or cash at the factory. For businesses and institutions we issue an invoice with VAT and 30-day payment terms once the account is approved.
233|Are your products safe for skin and the environment?
234|Our formulas comply with regulatory limits on active ingredients, with the pH checked on every batch. Products intended for hands are enriched with gentle agents; concentrated products such as bleach and degreaser must be used with gloves, following the dosages on the label. We favour recyclable packaging and offer 5 L refills to reduce plastic waste.
